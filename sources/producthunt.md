# Product Hunt Research Playbook

## Objectives
Identify commercial product launches, micro-SaaS trends, and new developer tools gaining early adopter traction.

## Monitored Categories
- Artificial Intelligence, Developer Tools, Productivity, Automation, SaaS.

## Evaluation
- Top #1–#3 Products of the Day in tech categories.
- Examine genuine comments vs upvote manipulation (check voter profile ages).
- Evaluate if the product represents a new market category or wrapper.
