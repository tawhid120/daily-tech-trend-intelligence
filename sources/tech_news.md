# Tech News & Media Playbook

## Objectives
Monitor reputable tech journalism outlets for major corporate moves, legal shifts, open-source controversies, and enterprise infrastructure deployments.

## Priority Outlets
- Tier 1: Ars Technica, The Verge, MIT Technology Review, Wired.
- Tier 2: TechCrunch, VentureBeat, 404 Media, The Register, InfoQ.

## Rules
- Distinguish between investigative tech journalism and sponsored promotional PR wire copies.
- Never count 20 syndicated articles about a single press release as 20 independent trends.
