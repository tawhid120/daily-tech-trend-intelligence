# Google Trends Research Playbook

## Objectives
Validate broad public and developer curiosity momentum through search volume shifts and breakout query alerts.

## Key Indicators
- **Breakout Queries:** Queries exhibiting >5,000% search growth over a 24-hour or 7-day period.
- **Comparative Volume:** Compare candidate tool search interest against established incumbents (e.g. comparing "Bun" vs "Node.js", or "LangGraph" vs "CrewAI").
